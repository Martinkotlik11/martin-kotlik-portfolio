Spot Welding, Riveting & Forging Practical Report

Hands-on manufacturing lab report covering resistance spot welding, specimen preparation, tensile testing, metallographic observation, riveting and forging operations.

Joining ProcessesMaterialsMechanical TestingManufacturing
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Joining and forming processes in practice

Spot welding

The report studies the principle and sizing of resistance spot welding, preparation of specimens and tensile testing for both single and double spot configurations. It also documents metallographic analysis to understand the weld nugget and failure behavior.

Riveting

Complementary work on riveting reviewed theoretical sizing rules and practical execution, reinforcing understanding of mechanical joining methods and process constraints.

Forging

The practical work also included forging activities, giving direct exposure to deformation processes and workshop practice within the Arts et Métiers manufacturing curriculum.

What this shows

This report highlights hands-on exposure to manufacturing processes, experimental work, interpretation of test results and the link between process parameters and mechanical behavior.
